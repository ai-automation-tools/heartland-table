# 🌾 The Heartland Table: A Mid-West Cookbook

> *From church basements to state fairs, from cast-iron skillets to grandmother's handwritten recipe cards — this is the food that built the Midwest.*

## About This Cookbook

**The Heartland Table** is a comprehensive celebration of Midwestern cuisine spanning 12 states: Iowa, Minnesota, Wisconsin, Illinois, Indiana, Ohio, Michigan, Nebraska, Kansas, Missouri, North Dakota, and South Dakota.

With ~90 recipes organized by category and enriched with cultural stories, regional rivalries, and farm-to-table spotlights, this cookbook captures not just what the Midwest eats — but *why*.

## 🎨 Design Guide

### Photography Style
- **Primary (70%):** Rustic Farmhouse — weathered wood, cast iron, natural light, linen
- **Secondary (30%):** Modern Editorial — clean, sharp focus, minimal styling

### Color Palette
- **Primary:** Ambers, warm wheats
- **Secondary:** Muted sage greens
- **Accent:** Deep cranberry / rustic red

### Typography
- **Titles & Body:** Classic serif — warmth and tradition
- **Subheadings & Features:** Clean sans-serif — modern contrast
